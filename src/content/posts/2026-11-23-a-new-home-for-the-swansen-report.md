The Swansen Report has a new home. This site replaces the old WordPress setup with something simpler and faster, built for the kind of reporting this publication does: Bayfield County government, life along the South Shore, and the decisions made in Madison that land here.

Stories are organized into three sections, which you'll find across the top of every page: **Bayfield County**, **The South Shore** and **Statewide**. Every story ends with a list of its sources, so you can check the work yourself.

The archive from the old site will move over in the coming weeks. Until then, past coverage remains available at [swansenreport.com](https://swansenreport.com).

If you read news in a feed reader, the new site has an [RSS feed](/rss.xml).

— Paul Swansen, editor and publisher
